# Read-only field and fitting result awareness

Status: first-target Stage 7.3 prototype; verified offline; live model evaluation pending.

## Generalizable lessons
When an application already exposes numerical objects underlying a plot, prefer a validated read-only adapter to visual inference from screenshots. Use explicit units, bounded sampling, deterministic aggregates and the actual originating configuration. Distinguish sampled peaks from exact extrema, numerical quadrature from analytical results, finite-domain integrals from whole-domain quantities, and display normalization from physical quantities. Avoid inferring energy or power fractions from an unrelated squared-field integral.

For fitting, keep the original optimization setup, completed result, latest attempt and current application state distinct. Report objective definition, weights, bounds, measured-versus-predicted residuals, optimizer status and available identifiability diagnostics. A good fit need not identify a unique physical model. A rank or condition diagnostic is not a statistical confidence interval, and local optimizer success is not a global optimum guarantee. Compare fitted-structure freshness separately from measurement identity and current optimizer settings.

## Local prototype
Stage 7.3 uses bounded analytical field sampling (64 radial midpoints and 16 angular samples per harmonic period per layer, at most 12 layers), eight radial magnitude samples per layer and deterministic finite-area squared-field integrals. It reuses the retained solution, never launches a new root solve, and invalidates cached summaries after normalization. The fit adapter captures originating inputs and completed-result provenance, reports bounded samples and full paired-grid statistics for included curves, and distinguishes cancellation from loss of retained evidence. Caps: 12 layers, 48 free parameters, eight curves and at most nine samples per curve. Two persisted read-only capabilities follow the previously user-approved default-on design; field access also requires single-mode result access. Session consent explicitly includes sampled fields and measured/predicted fitting samples. Paths are excluded. No new provider, execution or arbitrary filesystem access. Archive schema and contents unchanged: no new field/fit persistence or model-directed archive retrieval. Context is bounded and an oversized combined request is rejected.
